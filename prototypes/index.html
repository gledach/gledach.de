gledach.de · index redesign · three directions

The index becomes the signal story. The other four tools get their own pages.

Each prototype is a complete scrolling index for signal, built from one Refero style. Same content spine in all three, so the comparison is about the design rather than about the copy: the eleven collectors, why those eleven, the five pipeline stages, who consumes the result, how to point it at another market, the subdomain convention, then the rest of the shelf as links out to pages that do not exist yet.

Nothing here touches index.html or assets/site.css. Each file is self-contained and excluded from deployment.

Two things worth knowing before you pick

All three depart from the live design system. The oxblood accent, Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Serif and the ivory ground are not in any of them. That is what you asked for, and it means whichever one wins, CLAUDE.md needs rewriting rather than amending.

Two source styles specify photography this brand does not have. A borrowed oil painting or a stock photo standing in for evidence is the one move the content rules forbid, so in A and C that slot carries real output instead. Each file's head comment lists every deviation and why.
